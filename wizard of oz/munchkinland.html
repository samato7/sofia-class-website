Munchkinland

The house lands with a thump in a country bursting with color. Small, cheerful Munchkins gather around. The house has landed on the Wicked Witch of the East, so they call Dorothy a hero. Her silver shoes now belong to her. The Good Witch of the North tells her that only the Wizard of Oz in the Emerald City can send her home.

Put on the shoes.
