The Wicked Witch

The Witch sends wolves, crows, and winged monkeys after the group, and she captures Dorothy. She wants the silver shoes. But when she knocks Dorothy down in a temper, Dorothy throws a bucket of water, and the Witch melts away. The friends carry the broomstick back to the city.
