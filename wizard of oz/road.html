The Yellow Brick Road

Dorothy sets off down the yellow brick road. She frees a Scarecrow from his pole, and he wants a brain. She oils a rusty Tin Woodman, who wants a heart. She meets a Cowardly Lion, who wants courage. The four become friends, and each one proves, a little at a time, that they already have what they are looking for.
