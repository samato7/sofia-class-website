The Gray Prairie

Dorothy lives on a flat, gray Kansas farm with Auntie Em, Uncle Henry, and her little dog Toto. Everything is the color of dust. One day Uncle Henry spots a cyclone racing across the plains. Dorothy runs for the house, but Toto hides and she goes back for him. The wind lifts the whole house into the sky.
